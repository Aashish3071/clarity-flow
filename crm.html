Built for Solar Installers and Sales Teams

Selling solar isn't a simple one-click sale. It requires site inspections, utility bill calculations, quotation revisions, permit paperwork, and physical installation coordination.

The Bottleneck Solar Companies Face:

Reps receive leads from Facebook ads or referrals, but customer electricity bills and roof photos get lost in WhatsApp chats. Reps forget to follow up on quotes, and management has zero visibility into which deals are waiting for site surveys vs utility approvals.

  • ✓ Centralized Bill & Roof Attachments: Store customer electricity bills, Sanctioned Load (kW), roof measurement photos, and proposal PDFs directly in the customer file.
  • ✓ Site Survey Scheduling: Assign technical surveyors with scheduled dates. Surveyors can update inspection notes and measurements right from the field.
  • ✓ Quote Follow-up Automation: Automated alert to the sales rep 48 hours after sending the proposal so quotes are never forgotten.
  • ✓ Installation & Net Metering Stages: Track physical installation progress, net metering application filing, and final commissioning in one pipeline view.

Insurance Brokers & Agencies

Built for Insurance Brokers and Agents

In insurance, renewal retention is your most valuable revenue stream. Losing renewals because someone missed a spreadsheet date cuts your recurring commissions directly.

The Bottleneck Insurance Agencies Face:

Client records are scattered across different insurance provider portals. Agents forget to reach out before a policy expires, the client renews elsewhere, and commission is lost forever.

  • ✓ Complete Policy Database: Record policy numbers, vehicle registration, coverage amounts, and insurer names in one searchable system.
  • ✓ Automated 30/15/7 Day Renewal Alerts: Your agents get a prioritized daily list of policies expiring soon, complete with client phone numbers.
  • ✓ Family Grouping: Connect husbands, wives, and dependents under one household so you can cross-sell health, motor, and life policies effortlessly.
  • ✓ Claim Tracking: Record claim status, hospital paperwork, and surveyor inspections to deliver stellar client service during emergencies.

Clinics, Dental & Healthcare

Built for Clinics and Healthcare Practices

Empty appointment slots and forgotten patient follow-ups hurt your practice revenue and patient health outcomes.

The Bottleneck Clinics Face:

Front desk staff are swamped with incoming phone calls and WhatsApp messages. Patients forget appointments, doctors sit with empty chair time, and patients never return for routine checkups.

  • ✓ Instant Appointment Scheduling: Simple calendar with doctor and room availability that any receptionist can master in 5 minutes.
  • ✓ Automated Visit Reminders: Send automatic booking confirmations and 24-hour reminder messages via WhatsApp and SMS to reduce no-shows.
  • ✓ 6-Month Routine Recall: Automatically flag dental patients due for scaling or therapy patients due for review consultations.
  • ✓ Consultation Notes: Quick treatment summaries and prescription history linked directly to the patient's phone number.
